Questions?

If you have any questions about what is allowed or not allowed, please read our full license terms or contact us.

Can I use photos and videos from Pexels for commercial purposes?

Yes, all photos and videos on Pexels are free for commercial and personal use. You can use them in your projects, websites, apps, or any other medium without asking for permission or giving attribution.

Do I need to attribute the photographer?

Attribution is not required, but it's always appreciated. If you want to give credit, you can do so by linking back to Pexels and mentioning the photographer's name.

Can I edit the photos and videos?

Yes, you can modify, crop, resize, or edit the photos and videos from Pexels in any way you like. Be creative and make them your own!
